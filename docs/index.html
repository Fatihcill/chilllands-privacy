01

Your choices and legal grounds

Usage/performance sharing and error reports are off by default. The welcome checkbox enables both only when you confirm; Settings > Feedback lets you change each separately. Optional telemetry relies on your consent. Refusing or withdrawing it does not prevent play. Support correspondence is processed to respond to your request, based on legitimate interests or pre-contractual/contractual necessity where applicable.

02

Usage and performance

With usage sharing enabled, PostHog receives a random installation ID, session/event IDs, game version, screens and time spent, tutorial/progress and match events, loading/reconnection results, performance summaries, and broad OS/GPU/CPU/RAM/display/quality categories. These IDs link sessions and are pseudonymous, not fully anonymous. No advertising profiles, session replay or automatic screen recordings are enabled.

03

Error reports and exclusions

With error sharing enabled, Sentry receives a separate random ID, error type and code location, version, session health and up to 30 recent predefined actions. Telemetry payloads exclude player names, Steam IDs, chat, save files, file paths, raw exception messages, screenshots, recordings and memory dumps. A provider can still see the network IP when receiving a request even when the event payload masks it.

04

Feedback, local files and other services

The feedback form holds your text locally and opens a draft in your email app. Nothing is emailed automatically. If you send it, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose, through our Gmail support mailbox. Avoid sensitive information. Logs, crash reports, saves and preferences can remain on your device; they are not automatically attached. Steam separately handles accounts, purchases and platform/network services under Valve's privacy policy.

05

Retention

Pending telemetry is eligible for local cleanup after seven days when the game maintains its cache; switching a channel off clears its unsent queue. Preferences and data-ID receipts can remain locally for your choices and deletion requests. PostHog's published free-plan event-history window is one year; query-window expiry is not proof of physical deletion. Sentry's published Developer error-event period is 30 days; release-health summaries, counters and backups have separate schedules. We use those summaries only as needed to assess release stability. Support correspondence is kept to resolve and document requests, or meet legal obligations. Ask us about the applicable retention or deletion scope for your record.

06

Recipients and international processing

Telemetry uses PostHog Inc. (EU project) and Functional Software Inc., trading as Sentry (Germany-region project). Support mail uses Google/Gmail. This static policy is hosted through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); we add no page analytics or advertising cookies, but hosting providers may process request/security logs. EU telemetry endpoints do not mean every support, subprocessor or backup operation stays in the EU. Applicable international-transfer requirements remain relevant; contact us for information on the safeguards applying to your data. This notice does not certify that any particular transfer agreement has been executed.

07

Withdrawal, deletion and your rights

Turn either channel off in Settings > Feedback to stop future collection and clear pending data for it. Previously delivered data requires a separate request: use COPY DATA IDS and email the relevant current/previous IDs to our support address. Opting in again after withdrawal creates a new ID. Provider-side deletion may be asynchronous; we do not promise instant erasure of every backup or session summary. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection and the rights in KVKK Article 11, and complain to the KVKK or another competent authority. We respond within the applicable legal period (generally 30 days under KVKK or one month under GDPR, with lawful extensions explained). We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects solely by automation.

08

Children, changes and contact

If you are below the local age for independent consent, leave optional sharing off and ask your parent or guardian. Contact us about information shared by a child. Material changes to collection or purposes will be explained before applying them and renewed consent sought where required. Contact the data controller through the support email above for questions, requests and applicable safeguards.
